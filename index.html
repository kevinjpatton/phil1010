Course Description

This course invites you to examine the foundations of human existence. Is there an objective meaning to life waiting to be discovered, or is meaning something we must courageously invent for ourselves? We will navigate through teleology, nihilism, existentialism, and absurdism, reading works from Aristotle, Nietzsche, Camus, Sartre, and contemporary philosophers.
